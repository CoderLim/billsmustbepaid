## About this site

**billsmustbepaid.net** is an independent fan / community portal for the game **Bills Must Be Paid** by [Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/). We host a free browser playable build, plus source-checked guides, wiki notes, achievement lists, and version comparisons.

We are **not** Rike Games, and we are **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** Rike Games unless we say otherwise. Game software, art, audio, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

## What we publish

- A free browser embed so you can try the game without installing
- Guides and wiki pages that prefer first-party sources (press kit, Steam, developer announcements)
- Clear labels when advice comes from the community rather than the developer
- Legal pages (privacy, terms) and a contact channel for site issues

## What we do not claim

- We do not invent unofficial stats when first-party data is missing
- We do not speak for Rike Games
- In-game progress inside the embed is controlled by the upstream game host, not by this site's accounts

For questions about **this website** (broken pages, privacy requests, corrections): **support@billsmustbepaid.net**
