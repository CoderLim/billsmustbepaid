## Introduction

billsmustbepaid.net ("we", "us") operates a free browser portal for **Bills Must Be Paid**, a game developed by [Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect on this website and how we use it.

The embedded game is hosted by a third-party game CDN / Rike Games. When you play, that service may collect gameplay data under its own policies. We do not control the game iframe's internal analytics.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — If you create an account on this site (optional), we store your name, email address, and authentication credentials via our auth provider.
2. **Usage data** — Standard server and analytics logs: pages visited, browser type, approximate region, referral source, and timestamps.
3. **Cookies and similar technologies** — We use cookies for language preference (`PARAGLIDE_LOCALE`), theme selection, and session management. Advertising and analytics partners (described below) may also set or read cookies, use web beacons, and collect IP addresses or other identifiers.
4. **Support messages** — If you contact us at support@billsmustbepaid.net or submit a support ticket, we retain the content of your message to respond.

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** require an account to play the free embedded game.

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the website and embedded game player.
- Respond to support requests sent to support@billsmustbepaid.net.
- Measure traffic and improve page performance (aggregated analytics).
- Serve and measure advertising (including Google AdSense) on eligible content pages.
- Detect abuse, fraud, or technical issues.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

This site uses **Google AdSense** to show ads on selected content pages (guides, wiki, blog, and similar). We do **not** place Google ads on the homepage game-play area that embeds third-party game content.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies to:

- Measure ad performance and prevent fraud

You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps at [Google's advertising policies / privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and manage ad personalization at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
