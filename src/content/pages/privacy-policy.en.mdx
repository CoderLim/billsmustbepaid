## Introduction

billsmustbepaid.net ("we", "us") operates a free browser portal for **Bills Must Be Paid**, a game developed by [Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect on this website and how we use it.

The embedded game is hosted by Rike Games at `billsmustbepaid.rikegames.com`. When you play, that service may collect gameplay data under its own policies. We do not control the game iframe's internal analytics.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — If you create an account on this site (optional), we store your name, email address, and authentication credentials via our auth provider.
2. **Usage data** — Standard server and analytics logs: pages visited, browser type, approximate region, referral source, and timestamps.
3. **Cookies** — We use cookies for language preference (`PARAGLIDE_LOCALE`), theme selection, and session management. Third-party analytics or ad scripts, if enabled by the site administrator, may set additional cookies.
4. **Support messages** — If you contact us at support@billsmustbepaid.net or submit a support ticket, we retain the content of your message to respond.

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** require an account to play the free embedded game.

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the website and embedded game player.
- Respond to support requests sent to support@billsmustbepaid.net.
- Measure traffic and improve page performance (aggregated analytics only).
- Detect abuse, fraud, or technical issues.

## Third-Party Services

This site may load:

- **Embedded game** — `https://billsmustbepaid.rikegames.com/` (Rike Games)
- **Analytics** — e.g. Google Analytics or Plausible, if configured by the administrator
- **Advertising** — e.g. Google AdSense, if configured by the administrator

Each third party operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review [Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/) for game-specific data practices.

## Data Retention

Support tickets and account data are kept as long as needed to provide the service or comply with legal obligations. Analytics logs are retained according to the configured analytics provider's defaults.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact **support@billsmustbepaid.net** to make a request.

## Children's Privacy
