## Contact us

Use this page for **billsmustbepaid.net** site issues only.

**Email:** [support@billsmustbepaid.net](mailto:support@billsmustbepaid.net)

Please include:

- The page URL (if something is broken)
- Your browser and device (for playback or layout bugs)
- A short description of the problem or request

## Privacy requests

To access, correct, export, or delete personal data we hold about you, email **support@billsmustbepaid.net** with the subject line `Privacy request`. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## Game / developer support

We do not provide official support for **Bills Must Be Paid**. For the game, saves inside the embed, refunds on Steam, or press inquiries, contact [Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/bills-must-be-paid) or the storefront where you purchased or played the game.
