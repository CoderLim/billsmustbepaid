## Who made Bills Must Be Paid?

[Rike Games](https://rikegames.com/) is a two-person indie studio based in Frankfurt, Germany. According to their [official press kit](https://rikegames.com/press/bills-must-be-paid/), the team has shipped mobile games since 2019 and web games since 2024, with more than **500,000 mobile downloads** and **20 million web plays** across their catalog. Bills Must Be Paid is their latest incremental title — described on Steam as an "active incremental game" rather than an idle one.

## The core loop: smash, earn, pay, upgrade

Each run follows the same pressure cycle:

1. **Smash piggy banks** — move your hammer with the mouse. The hammer swings while you hover; every swing drains stamina.
2. **Collect loot** — broken piggies drop coins and random items. Some piggy types pay more, restore stamina, or behave unpredictably.
3. **Pay bills on time** — recurring bills arrive with deadlines. Pay them and pick **one of three perk cards** (for example Deep Freeze, Recovery Smash, or Interest Rate). Miss a payment and the debt collector takes a cut of your earnings (official sources do not publish a fixed percentage).
4. **Invest leftovers** — spend coins on the [skill tree](/wiki/skill-tree 'Bills Must Be Paid skill tree guide') or new [hammers](/wiki/hammers 'Hammer upgrades in Bills Must Be Paid'). Declare bankruptcy to convert progress into legacy points for permanent rings and bracelets.

That loop is what itch.io players rated **4.5/5** and CrazyGames scored **9.5/10** — short sessions with real decisions, not background clicking.

## Why it is not an idle game

Idle games progress while you are away. Bills Must Be Paid demands attention every second:

- **Stamina is finite.** You cannot smash forever — you choose which [piggy bank](/wiki/piggy-banks 'Piggy bank types and loot') is worth the swing.
- **Bills force timing.** Hoarding coins feels safe until a deadline passes and the collector taxes everything you earn.
- **Perks change the next run.** Paying a bill is not just survival — it is a build choice.

Rike Games calls the genre "active incremental." Think incremental roguelite: each run feeds the next through upgrades, collections, and (on Steam) a deeper prestige loop after bankruptcy.

## Piggy banks, hammers, and long-term goals

### Piggy bank types

Official descriptions highlight behavior differences, not just skins:

| Piggy           | What players notice                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Normalito**   | Slow and predictable — a reliable baseline target      |
| **The Tourist** | Big and lazy, but restores stamina when smashed        |
| **Woody**       | Barely moves — easy to hit, lower urgency              |
| **Piñata**      | Random drops: one coin or a whole stack                |
| **El Loco**     | Wanders, zigzags, and charges — high risk, high reward |

Rarer types unlock as you progress. The Piggies collection screen tracks every type you have met — a completion goal that keeps runs fresh.

### Hammers and the skill tree

Each hammer differs in damage, swing speed, crit chance, and hit radius. Some hit hard in a small area; others swing fast with wide coverage. The skill tree branches into grip strength, caffeine addiction, gym training, wrist enhancement, luck, and wilder late-game nodes like rock rain and electrified hammers.

Steam adds **27 achievements**, rare coin collections, rings, bracelets, desk gadgets, and risk mechanics on top of this base loop.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Bills Must Be Paid free?

Yes. The browser build on this site is completely free with no sign-up. Steam offers a free demo; the full game is a paid release with more content.
